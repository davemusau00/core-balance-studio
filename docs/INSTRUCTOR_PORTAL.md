# Core Balance Studio — Instructor & Staff Portal Guide

The **Instructor Portal** (`/instructor`) provides teaching staff with real-time class tools, Reformer bed maps, client medical notes, and earnings tracking.

---

## 🔑 1. Quick Staff Authentication

- **Demo Staff Access**: On the Sign-In page (`/signin`), click **"Instructor"** to log in instantly as *Amara Osei (Senior Instructor)*.
- **Direct Portal Route**: Authenticated instructors land directly on `/instructor`.

---

## 📋 2. Roster & Reformer Bed Assignments

Every assigned class session displays an active student roster:
- **Bed Numbers (01–12)**: Maps each attendee to their selected Reformer machine on the studio floor.
- **1-Tap Check-in**: Tap **"Mark Present"** to confirm attendance. Checked-in clients display a green badge.
- **Membership Pack**: View whether the student is on a *10 Class Pack*, *Unlimited Monthly*, or *5 Class Pack*.

---

## ⚠️ 3. Clinical & Injury Warning System

Instructors have instant visibility into client physical restrictions before teaching:
- **Warning Banners**: Highlighted amber banners appear above student cards for medical tags such as:
  - *Lumbar disc herniation (L4-L5)* — Avoid deep spinal flexion.
  - *Right shoulder impingement* — Keep arm spring tension light.
  - *Prenatal Trimester 2* — Utilize wedge pillows for supine work.
- **Adding/Editing Notes**: Click **"Add Clinical Note"** or **"Edit"** on any student card to update notes in real-time.

---

## 🔄 4. Shift Coverage & Sub Requests

If an instructor cannot teach a scheduled session:
1. Click **"Request Shift Cover (Sub)"** at the top of the dashboard.
2. Enter the reason for the sub request.
3. Click **"Send Request"** to broadcast the shift cover alert to available instructors and studio administrators.

---

## 💰 5. Monthly Earnings Tracking

The dashboard header displays:
- **Classes Taught**: Completed session count for the current month.
- **Instructor Rating**: Average client feedback rating (e.g. 4.9 ★).
- **Estimated Earnings**: Real-time commission total (Base Rate + Per-Head Bonus).
